Scale cap for this measurement class (QSR v0 §7): unquantized baseline arm <= 16.5 GB on disk (~8B-class) in CPU RAM; both arms under one pinned llama.cpp binary, CPU-only.
Measured over 40 curated probes. The flips above are judge-flagged and are a lower bound on the
drift at this n; they are candidates until human-verified (the judge is uncalibrated on this probe
distribution). This report does not certify safety in either direction.
The floor runs the WRONG WAY on detection: at the assumed zero judge error the rejection
threshold is the smallest it can be, so a flip is EASIEST to declare here, and the alpha behind
it is a lower bound on the true type-I risk rather than a bound on it. Verify the flipped pairs.
The MDE is a PERFECT-JUDGE FLOOR: it assumes a judge that never errs, so it is a lower
bound on this run's resolution, not the resolution. No measured judge error (epsilon) has
been folded into it — and the amount it is off by is NOT unknown. quantfit's own judge was
measured in-distribution (n=80, single-rater): per-arm epsilon 0.196, false-flip bound 0.391,
at which the effective MDE is 1.0 for every n <= 34. At the n above, no effect size is
detectable. Treat the number in that column as a floor over a resolution that is not yet shown.
